# AscensionLib: wiki handoff

Generated by `tools/build_guide.py` from `design/guide.json`, the design data and the code, so every number matches the rules. Do not edit by hand: change the source and rebuild. Each `##` section is one wiki page.

**About the mod.** AscensionLib is a server-side Fabric mod for Cobblemon (Minecraft 1.21.1). Every Pokémon gets a rarity and modifiers that change how it fights, and players grow them with upgrades, materials and rare items. Screens are web pages shown by the WebGUI mod on the client. It is the shared library for CobbleRaids, CobbleTowers and ascensionDungeons.

## Quick start

*Every Pokémon gets a rarity and traits. You grow it instead of replacing it.*

**At a glance:** Catch it, then Inspect it in your Dex; Every 10 levels: one free upgrade; Earn materials in Towers, dungeons and raids; Win battles to promote its rarity; Scout enemies before you fight; At the top: Uniques and Fusion.

Every Pokémon you catch or hatch gets an ascension profile: a rarity and a few traits that change how it fights. You grow them instead of replacing them. Here is the short version.

1. Catch or hatch a Pokémon. Right-click your AscensionDex (get one with /ascend dex), pick it and press Inspect to see its rarity and traits.
2. Level it up. Every 10 levels earns one upgrade credit (up to 10). Spend it on the Traits screen (Upgrade): one trait goes up a rank and rerolls its value. It never fails.
3. Earn materials. Tower runs, trials, dungeons and raids pay Resonance Dust, Facets, Cores and more into your wallet (the Wallet tab of the Dex).
4. Win battles with it. A Pokémon that fights in a victory earns 3 attunement. Attunement plus dust, facets and cores promotes it to the next rarity, all the way to Radiant.
5. Scout enemies. A Scouter reveals an enemy's rarity and traits before you commit: use the opponent panel on the overlay (press WebGUI's interact key to click it) or /ascend scout.
6. Go deep. At rank V you can Perfect and Overreach a trait; a Catalyst installs a Unique power; two Pokémon that each hold a Unique can be bonded at a Fuser into a Transcendent.

> **Tip:** Every page has a − and + in its header to make it bigger or smaller. It is remembered for you.

## How it fits with Cobblemon

*Nature, IVs and EVs work as always. Ascension adds rarity and traits on top.*

**At a glance:** Rarity sets how many traits it holds (1 to 6); Traits are battle bonuses you upgrade; Rarity has nothing to do with IVs or shininess; IVs and EVs stay on Cobblemon's Summary.

Everything you know from Cobblemon still works. Ascension adds one layer on top: rarity decides how many traits a Pokémon can hold, and traits are battle bonuses you upgrade over time.

| System | What it is | Set when | How it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Raises one stat and lowers another | Caught or hatched | Cobblemon's usual items |
| IVs | Its genes, 0 to 31 per stat | Caught or hatched | Cobblemon's usual items |
| EVs | Training from battles | Battling | Cobblemon's usual items |
| Rarity | How many trait slots it has (1 to 6) | Caught in a profile zone, or an Ascension Sigil | Promote it with attunement and materials. It never goes down |
| Traits | Battle bonuses in those slots | Filled when a slot opens | Upgrade, Refine and Reforge on the Traits screen |

> **Tip:** Rarity has nothing to do with IVs, shininess or whether a species is legendary. A perfect-IV Pokémon can roll Common, and a Common can be promoted all the way to Radiant. Check IVs, EVs and nature on Cobblemon's Summary screen as usual.

## Rarity and traits

*Rarity sets how many traits a Pokémon holds. Each trait ranks from I to V.*

**At a glance:** Attack traits: more damage dealt; Guard traits: less damage taken, or more healing; A higher rank rolls a higher range; Traits of one theme boost each other.

Rarity decides how many trait slots a Pokémon has, from 1 to 6. Attack traits boost the damage it deals; Guard traits cut the damage it takes or boost its healing. Every Pokémon can eventually reach Radiant.

| Rarity | Trait slots | Layout | Base chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 | 1 attack, 0 guard | 55.0% |
| Uncommon | 2 | 1 attack, 1 guard | 28.0% |
| Rare | 3 | 2 attack, 1 guard | 12.0% |
| Epic | 4 | 2 attack, 2 guard | 4.0% |
| Awakened | 5 | 3 attack, 2 guard | 0.90% |
| Radiant | 6 | 3 attack, 3 guard | 0.10% |

Each trait has a rank from I to V. Rank I is its base range; every later rank starts one above the last and is about half as wide as rank I, so rank V tops out near three times rank I. A value is always a whole percent.

Bonuses stack inside five channels, each with a cap so no team becomes unbeatable: outgoing damage 100%, damage reduction 50%, healing 50%, damage over time 100% and mechanics 50%.

Two traits of the same family never share a Pokémon. Themes (Tempo, Toxin, Predator, Bulwark, Vitality) strengthen each other: two traits of one theme give x1.15, three or more x1.30. The inspector shows the theme strip.

## Growing a Pokémon

*Upgrade, reroll and promote on the Traits screen. Nothing is spent until you confirm.*

**At a glance:** Upgrade: free, one rank up, never fails; Refine: reroll the value; Reforge: reroll the trait; Promote: attunement and materials add a slot.

Open the Traits screen with the Traits button, from the Dex or Inspect. Nothing is spent until you press Review and then Confirm.

| Action | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Upgrade | Raise one trait one rank and reroll its value in the new, higher range. Never fails. | 1 upgrade credit (free) |
| Refine | Reroll one trait's value inside its current rank. It can come out lower. | 12 dust |
| Reforge | Reroll which trait one slot holds. The rank stays and the value rerolls; the same trait can come back. | 24 dust + 1 facet |
| Perfect | Rank V only. Raise the lowest value the trait can roll by one point, up to its range top. A Refine can never roll below the floor. | 30 dust, +10 for each step already bought on that slot |
| Overreach | Rank V at its range top only. One gamble per trait: 45% for a permanent value 10 to 25% above the range top; otherwise the value drops into the lower half. Either way the slot is sealed (only a Reforge changes it). | 60 dust + 2 facets + 1 Tower Mark |
| Promote | Move the whole Pokémon up one rarity and open one new trait slot. All traits are kept. | see the promotion table, plus attunement |

| Promotion | Cost | Attunement needed |
|---|---|---|
| Common to Uncommon | 20 dust | 3 |
| Uncommon to Rare | 50 dust, 2 facets | 10 |
| Rare to Epic | 100 dust, 5 facets | 25 |
| Epic to Awakened | 200 dust, 10 facets, 2 cores | 50 |
| Awakened to Radiant | 400 dust, 20 facets, 5 cores | 90 |

Attunement is earned only by Pokémon that fought: 3 for each victory over a Tower's floor 5 boss, a trial, a dungeon or a raid. Clearing a Tower's champion pays 12 to 22 more, rising with the run's difficulty, so a whole Tower is worth 15 to 25. A Pokémon with no profile earns nothing and you are told so; an Ascension Sigil gives it one.

> **Important:** Overreach is a point of no return. A Pokémon with an Overreached slot can never use an Hourglass of Undoing.

## Uniques and Transcendents

*A Unique is one strong power with a drawback. Fusion turns two holders into a Transcendent.*

**At a glance:** A Catalyst and 1 Tower Mark install one; One Unique per Pokémon; Fuse two at a Project Fusion Fuser; A Transcendent's power is permanent.

A Unique is one powerful ability with a real drawback. It needs a Unique Catalyst, which you assemble from 100 Unique Fragments, and 1 Tower Mark. A Pokémon holds one Unique; installing a different one replaces it.

| Unique | Benefit | Drawback |
|---|---|---|
| Ashen Heart | Burns you inflict deal 50% more damage. | Your moves deal 15% less damage. |
| Last Breath | Once per battle, survive a lethal hit with 1 HP. | You receive half as much healing. |
| Creeping Venom | Poison you inflict grows 20% stronger each turn, up to +80%. | You take 20% more damage from Psychic moves. |
| Stormcaller | While weather is active, your moves of its matching type deal 30% more damage (Fire in sun, Water in rain). | With no weather active, your moves deal 15% less damage. |
| Rupture | Your physical moves always cause bleeding. | Bleed you cause is weaker per stack: 100%, 80%, 60%. |
| Titan's Heart | Your super-effective moves deal bonus damage equal to 10% of your max HP. | You cannot be healed in battle. |
| 777 | Item rewards you earn are 20% larger while it is in your party. | You take 25% more damage from moves. |
| Crescendo | Your moves deal 8% more for each full turn you have stayed in battle, up to 40%. Switching out resets it. | For your first two turns after entering, your moves deal 20% less. |
| Nightfang | Your damaging moves heal you 25% of the damage they deal. | You take 30% more damage from Fire and Fairy moves. |
| Iron Maiden | An enemy that hits you with a contact move loses 1/8 of its max HP. | Your own contact moves deal 20% less damage. |
| Tailwind Heart | Your Speed is 50% higher. | Your Defense and Sp. Def are 15% lower. |
| Huntmark | Your first hit marks a foe; your later moves against it deal 25% more until it leaves the field. | You cannot land critical hits. |
| Martyr's Gift | When you faint, the next ally to enter heals 50% of its max HP and gains +1 Attack and +1 Sp. Atk. | You take 15% more damage from moves. |
| Aegis Oath | You take 30% less damage from moves. | Your moves deal 30% less damage. |
| Fickle Fortune | Each turn a coin is flipped: heads, your moves deal 40% more that turn. | Tails: your moves deal 20% less that turn. |

A Transcendent is made at a Fuser, the lab machine from Project Fusion. Two Pokémon that each hold a Unique are fused for good: one becomes the body, the other lives on inside it as the head, and their two Uniques become one completely new power. It can never be separated.

1. Put both Pokémon in your party and open a Fuser. Choose the body and the head, then press Fuse. The Fuser only takes two Pokémon that each hold a Unique.
2. The Transcendence page opens on that pair. The body is the host, the head is the donor; Swap trades them. Read the new power, its drawback and the price.
3. Press Review Transcendence, then Transcend for good. Or press Plain fusion to fuse them without a Transcendence, for Project Fusion's own fee; a plain fusion can be separated later.
4. The Fuser works for a moment. Collect the result by right-clicking the Fuser with a Poké Ball. A Transcendent is revealed as you collect it, and you can share it in chat.

- Host (the body): Radiant, every upgrade credit spent, at least 150 attunement, holding a Unique.
- Donor (the head): Epic or better, holding a different Unique, and not already a Transcendent.
- Price: 600 dust, 30 facets, 10 cores, 1 Catalyst and 5 Tower Marks.
- If the Fuser is interrupted (the server stops while it works), both Pokémon come back and the price is refunded.
- Genome Shift still works on a Transcendent: it swaps head and body, and the power's attack and defence types swap with them.
- Inspect any of your Pokémon to see how close it is to being a host or a donor, and which named recipes its species belongs to.
- A Transcendent's power cannot be replaced, and a Transcendent bond can never be separated.

## Materials and your wallet

*Your materials live in your wallet. Loot you pick up joins it by itself.*

**At a glance:** Take materials out as tradable vouchers; A 3% fee on each withdrawal; Deposit vouchers to get them back; Tower Marks are bound to you.

Materials live in your wallet, which is saved with the world. Loot that drops as an item joins the wallet by itself when you carry it. Open it from the Wallet tab of the AscensionDex, or the Wallet button on the Traits screen.

| Item | Rarity | What it is for | Trade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resonance Dust | Common | Raw resonance, spent on upgrades, refining and perfecting. | Tradable |
| Facet | Uncommon | A cut crystal that sharpens an affix. Spent on reforging and overreaching. | Tradable |
| Ascension Core | Epic | The heart of a promotion. Earned in the Tower. | Tradable |
| Apex Shard | Epic | A crown of crystal won from raid bosses. Needed for the highest promotions. | Tradable |
| Unique Fragment | Rare | Collect enough to assemble a Unique Catalyst. | Tradable |
| Unique Catalyst | Awakened | Installs or replaces a Unique power. | Tradable |
| Scouter | Uncommon | Reveals one enemy's rating. | Tradable |
| Ascension Sigil | Epic | Gives a Pokemon with no profile a rolled rarity. | Tradable |
| Tower Mark | Rare | Proof of a Tower clear. Bound to you; it cannot be traded. | Bound to you |

| Unbinding Prism | Awakened | Swaps a Pokemon's Unique for another, with no Catalyst and no Tower Mark. | Tradable |

Take currency out as items to trade it, then redeem the items with Deposit. A 3% fee, rounded up, is paid on top of a withdrawal. Tower Marks are bound to you and can never be taken out.

> **Tip:** A voucher is a bearer item: whoever holds it can deposit it, and each batch can only ever be redeemed for what it still holds, so a copied stack is worth nothing.

## Towers and completion chests

*Towers are the only place that pays Cores. Clear bosses for chests; play clean for a better tier.*

**At a glance:** Floor 5 boss: a mini chest; Champion: a completion chest and its crown; Score: cards taken, cycles, no faints; The first 20 chests a day pay in full.

A Tower is where Cores come from, and nowhere else pays them. Clearing the first boss (floor 5) opens a mini chest; clearing a cycle's champion (floor 10, and every Ascension cycle after) opens a completion chest.

The completion chest has three parts: a guaranteed pile of dust, facets, fragments, scouters and Tower Marks; a quality tier that multiplies it; and a crown of one to three rolls of something notable. The tier is earned by difficulty and a clean run, never by speed.

Score = challenge cards taken + 2 per Ascension cycle + 2 if no Pokémon fainted in the cycle.

| Tier | Reached at | Pile | Guaranteed pile | Crown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bronze | score 0+ | x0.7 | 560 dust, 4 facets, 2 fragments, 4 scouters, 3 Marks | 1 crown roll (30% Core each) |
| Silver | score 2+ | x1 | 800 dust, 6 facets, 3 fragments, 4 scouters, 3 Marks | 1 crown roll (34% Core each) |
| Gold | score 5+ | x1.5 | 1200 dust, 9 facets, 5 fragments, 4 scouters, 4 Marks | 2 crown rolls (32% Core each) |
| Mythic | score 8+ | x2.25 | 1800 dust, 14 facets, 7 fragments, 4 scouters, 5 Marks | 3 crown rolls (31% Core each) |

The crown can hold an Ascension Core, a bundle of 12 Unique Fragments, an Ascension Sigil, a Unique Catalyst, a Tower armor piece, a CobbleCards pack, an Hourglass of Undoing or an Unbinding Prism. A completion never ends without one.

The mini chest at floor 5 pays 200 dust, 1 facet, 1 fragment, 1 scouter, 1 Tower Mark and a 5% chance of a Core.

> **Important:** Daily limit: your first 20 chests of a day (UTC) pay in full, then each chest pays 25% of its dust. Everything else is always paid in full. It resets at midnight UTC.

Dungeons and trials pay smaller amounts of dust and facets, never Cores. Raids keep their own rewards but still give attunement.

## In battle

*Traits work inside normal battle rules, against wild Pokémon, trainers, Towers and raids.*

**At a glance:** Fights between players stay native; A Scouter reveals an enemy's traits; Bleeding shows as a red badge on the overlay; Battle effects need CobbleRaids.

Traits act inside the battle simulator, so they follow the normal rules: immunities, abilities and caps all still apply. They take effect in Tower and raid fights and in every fight against a wild Pokémon or a trainer's Pokémon, which have traits of their own: the deeper the place (a dungeon, say), the rarer they tend to be. Fights between players are native for both sides. A Scouter reveals the wild Pokémon you are looking at. Battle effects need CobbleRaids installed.

- Scouter: reveals one enemy's rarity and traits, once per enemy per encounter. Use the opponent panel on the overlay (press WebGUI's interact key to click it) or run /ascend scout. Look at a wild Pokémon inside a zone and run /ascend scout look.
- Bleed: some traits and Uniques cause bleeding, about 8% of max HP per stack each turn, up to 3 stacks for 3 turns. A red badge on the overlay shows who is bleeding, how many stacks and how many turns are left.
- Cobblemon's own statuses (burn, poison and the rest) work as normal; Smoldering and Venomous strengthen your own burns and poison.

## Respec items

*Two rare items undo an investment: the Hourglass resets ranks, the Prism swaps a Unique.*

**At a glance:** Hourglass: every trait back to rank I; Your upgrade credits come back; Prism: swap a Unique for free; Materials are never refunded.

Two rare items undo an investment. They drop from Tower completion chests, and the Hourglass can also be bought with Tower Marks. Both are tradable. Use them in the Respec tab (Hourglass) or the Unique tab (Prism) of the Traits screen.

| Item | Effect | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Hourglass of Undoing | Every trait goes back to rank I with a fresh value and its perfected floor cleared, and every upgrade credit comes back to spend again. Traits, Unique, rarity and attunement stay. No materials are refunded. | About 1 in 11 completions, or 90 Tower Marks |
| Unbinding Prism | Swap a Pokémon's Unique for a different one with no Catalyst and no Tower Mark. | About 1 in 34 completions |

> **Important:** A Pokémon that has Overreached can never be respecced: the Hourglass is refused and not used up. A Transcendent's power cannot be swapped.

## Help and troubleshooting

*Quick fixes for the common problems.*

- A page will not open: every screen is a web page shown by the WebGUI mod, so your client needs WebGUI installed. The server tells you when yours is missing.
- Items look like plain vanilla items: the server's resource pack gives them their art. Accept it when you join; without it they still work.
- A Pokémon says "No profile": you had it before the mod, it was traded or given to you, or it was caught in a place where the server has profiles switched off. An Ascension Sigil gives it a profile with a rolled rarity (Inspect, then Use Ascension Sigil).
- A Pokémon is locked: Tower rental Pokémon are on loan and cannot be changed.
- A craft button is greyed out: hover it, or read the red line beside it, for the exact reason (a missing material, a rank, a seal).
- Nothing happened after I confirmed: every action is checked and committed once by the server. If you see "already used" it had already gone through; reopen the page to see the result.

## Server administration

AscensionLib is a server-side Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 with Cobblemon 1.8.1 or 1.8.2. Clients need only the WebGUI mod (no client mod of ours). CobbleRaids makes battle effects act in fights; CobbleTowers and ascensionDungeons pay rewards through the library's contract.

- Data: each world keeps its progression in <world>/cobbleascend/progression.db (SQLite) with an authority.id marker; the world and the database belong together.
- Backups: a snapshot is taken at server start, then daily, into <world>/cobbleascend/backups/ (the newest 14 are kept), and /ascend admin backup takes one now. Before a schema upgrade the mod copies the store beside itself as progression.db.before-schemaN-fromM-<time>.bak and refuses to upgrade if it cannot.
- Resource pack: the pack in build/AscensionLib-resourcepack.zip gives the items their art. Host it and set resource-pack and resource-pack-sha1 in server.properties, or, with Polymer, add it to config/polymer/resource-pack.json under include_zips and enable auto-host.
- Page sizes are saved per player in <world>/ascensionlib-ui.json.
- Tuning: config/ascensionlib.json (written with every default on the first start) holds the economy's dials: the daily budget, the withdrawal fee, the Hourglass price, attunement per victory, the mini chest and the whole completion chest. Edit it and run /ascend admin reload, which reports what changed. The in-game guide shows the server's own numbers.
- Admin commands (permission level 2): /ascend admin grant <player> <material> <amount>, backup, reload, inspect [slot], craft [slot], fuse [slot], sigil <slot>, wallet [withdraw|deposit], attunement <slot> <points>, initialize <slot>, completion <tier> [pay], reveal, acquire, rate-target.
