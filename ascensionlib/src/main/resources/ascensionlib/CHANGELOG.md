# AscensionLib changelog

## 1.0.0+fabric (2026-11-23)

### New
- Abandoned Laboratories: ruined underground labs now appear in the overworld, with guards inside. Every container in a lab pays each player once, so nobody has to race for it.
- Fuser bond: with Cobblemon Project Fusion installed, two Pokemon that each hold a Unique can be fused at its Fuser into one Pokemon and one Transcendent, for good. The Fuser will not take a rented Pokemon.
- Encounter payouts now arrive as voucher items. Right-click a voucher to deposit it into your wallet, or trade it. Waiting vouchers are handed over every few seconds and when you log in.
- Prizes from other mods, such as the CobbleTowers Mystery Bag, can be paid as vouchers the same way.
- Trait tutorials: the first time you open a trait mechanic, a short card explains what it does, what it costs and what it risks. Got it dismisses it for good, and the ? button brings it back.
- The Traits screen has a new tab rail grouped by purpose, each tab with its own icon and colour. Refine shows its value inside the rank's range, Overreach shows a win and lose bar, and Promote shows the six-tier ladder.
- The capture card now shows how many of your six trait slots are filled, marks each trait as Attack or Guard, shows an IV line and has an Inspect button.
- A new guide topic, How it fits with Cobblemon, explains what Ascension adds on top of Cobblemon's own stats.
- Pokemon found in Ascension Dungeons chests now get a rating based on the dive's zone tier, never lower than the dive's difficulty allows.

### Changed
- The top two rarities are now called Radiant and Ascended instead of Legendary and Mythical, so they no longer clash with Cobblemon's own species groups. Pokemon you already have keep their rarity.
- Modifiers are now called traits everywhere: the capture card, the Inspect screen, the Craft screen, the guide, item tooltips and the fusion and promotion messages. Traits sit in Attack and Guard slots.
- The Inspect screen has a Traits header and a Base stats label, and points to Cobblemon's Summary screen for IVs, EVs and nature.
