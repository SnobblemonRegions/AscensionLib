Rarity in Cobblemon terms

An audit of how the capture reveal, the inspect screen and the guide explain rarity and modifiers, with three concepts that present them in language a Cobblemon player already knows. The mechanics stay the same. Only the words and their placement change.

Sources audited: capture.js, inspect.html, guide-data.js, ui.js. Mockups use sample data (a Rare Growlithe) and a placeholder where the in-game card sprite would sit.

What gets in the way today

HIGHOne concept has four names. A player can't tell that a "trait", a "modifier", an "affix" and a "prefix" are the same kind of thing. Capture: ASCENSION TRAITS DISCOVERED, then MODIFIERS (3) once there are 3+. Inspect header: Affixes. Guide: "modifier" throughout, "affix" in the Facet and Respec text. Use one word everywhere: Trait. The capture card already uses it, and Cobblemon doesn't use the word for anything else.

Rarity enum LEGENDARY, MYTHICAL. Shown as Mythic (A.rarityName), but stored and styled as mythical. A decision for you (see the end of this page). Whatever you pick, use the same spelling everywhere.
HIGHNothing connects rarity to what players already know. Cobblemon players think in IVs, nature and EVs. Neither screen says how rarity relates to them, or what rarity actually decides. Capture: Every great partner starts with potential. Guide intro: "a rarity and a few modifiers that change how it fights". One sentence everywhere: IVs are what it was born with. MED"Prefix" and "suffix" are ARPG grammar. They name where a word sits, not what the trait does. Inspect: Prefix 2/2 · Suffix 1/1. Guide table: "2 prefix, 1 suffix". Attack trait and Guard trait, matching what the guide already says they do (offense, then defense and recovery).
MEDToo many header labels on the capture card compete with the one thing that matters (what you caught and what it rolled), and the main button's verb is vague. LOWThe rarity progress bar says how far up the ladder you are, but not what the next step gives you. Capture: 6-segment bar labelled COMMON … MYTHIC. Show it as trait slots (3 of 6 filled), so promotion reads as "one more trait".
LOWSome descriptions use internal jargon. Inspect: A Unique power: fixed tuning, no ordinary rank. Fallbacks: An offensive modifier. / A defensive or recovery modifier. "A Unique power. It has no ranks and can't be upgraded." Fallbacks: "Boosts this Pokémon's attacks." / "Protects or heals this Pokémon."

rarity (unexplained)
Rarity = trait slots

Always shown with its slot count: "Rare · 3 trait slots".

GUIDEHow it fits with Cobblemon

Everything you know from Cobblemon still works. Ascension adds one more layer on top: rarity decides how many traits a Pokémon can hold, and traits are small battle bonuses you upgrade over time.

SystemWhat it isSet whenCan you change it?
Nature±10% to two statsCaught or hatchedMints
IVsIts genes, 0–31 per statCaught or hatchedBottle Caps (if your server has them)
EVsTraining, up to 510BattlingVitamins, EV-reducing berries
RarityHow many trait slots (1–6)Caught in a zone, or a SigilPromote with attunement and materials. It never goes down
TraitsBattle bonuses in those slotsFilled when a slot opensUpgrade, Refine, Reforge in Craft

Rarity has nothing to do with IVs, shininess or whether a species is legendary. A perfect-IV Pokémon can roll Common, and a Common can be promoted all the way up.

3 · Guide: "How it fits with Cobblemon"

  • A new first guide page that teaches by comparison. The two highlighted rows are the only new ideas, and they sit inside a table players already understand.
  • The last line says outright what DESIGN.md decided: rarity is independent of IVs and shininess. That heads off the "why is my 6IV Pokémon Common?" question.
  • The Cobblemon rows need checking against the version your server runs before shipping (Mints, Bottle Caps and vitamins vary by version and config).

Your decision: the top two tier names

This is the one finding I can't fix with wording alone, because the tier names are product terms. Here are three options:

A · Keep them

COMMONUNCOMMONRAREEPICLEGENDARYMYTHIC

No code or data changes. The guide's last line has to carry the "not the same as a Legendary Pokémon" message.

Least work, and the confusion stays.

B · Rename the top two

COMMONUNCOMMONRAREEPICRADIANTASCENDED

Display names only. The stored ids (legendary, mythical) stay, so saved profiles are untouched. "Ascended" ties the top tier to the mod's name.

My pick. It removes the clash and needs no save migration.

C · Stars instead of words

1 STAR23456 STARS

Matches the slot count exactly (3 stars = 3 traits), like Tera Raid star ratings. But it loses the names players already use in chat, and it collides with raid difficulty stars if CobbleRaids shows them.

Clearest number, weakest identity.
